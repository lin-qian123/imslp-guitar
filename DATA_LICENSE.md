annotations, documentation, and visual assets are released under the
[Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International License](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/),
unless a source record or linked page states otherwise.

Source titles, composer names, category names, work identifiers, and source
URLs are retained to identify and attribute records from the
[International Music Score Library Project](https://imslp.org/). IMSLP page
content has its own license and attribution requirements. Facts and public
domain material may not be copyrightable in every jurisdiction.

The public catalog does not redistribute score files. Every score remains
subject to the copyright status and license shown on its IMSLP work page and in
the file itself. Nothing in this repository grants additional rights to those
files.
